## EN

🐾 Pets & minions — 32.4.beta.8, from your beta feedback:

• 🚪 Doors close behind you again (heap-wedging stays — it's a tactic).
• 🗺️ No more permanently lit map spots after descending.
• 👥 No more duplicated pets/statues after transitions and reloads.
• 🕷️ Spider workers and Moongrace no longer clone your pets into a feral copy.
• 💪 Pet strength persists in saves; heavy armor slows pets too.
• 📋 Orders survive burning, gas and traps — damage over time doesn't cancel them.
• 🛡️ New "stay here" order: a pet on its own cell guards that post.
• 👣 Ordering a pet onto your cell means "follow me", not "attack me".
• ⚔️ Pets fight at full strength unwatched; their deaths are announced.
• 🤝 No more offers to steal from your own pet.

Also: pet windows now fit any screen and scroll.

As usual, beta: most of these bugs came in with earlier 32.4 betas; old saves work, but strange old-save pets may vanish.
Beta: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.nyrds.pixeldungeon.ml

## Promo picture — txt2img (Qwen-Image)

Concept: the pets round — the hero's loyal squad holding a sewer doorway
against the cloning spider. Built from docs/txt2img_promo_library.md blocks:
Sewers SCENE, Warrior CAST + pet-tagged marsupial rat and animated statue,
hostile spider worker as the threat, torchlit LIGHTING, banner COMPOSITION,
shared STYLE/CONSTRAINTS/negative verbatim.

### Main prompt

```
A 16-bit pixel art promotional banner for a retro dungeon crawler game, wide
landscape composition.

SCENE: a dungeon sewer level of grey-green cobblestone and brick slick with
slime, dark still water pools reflecting torchlight, moss in wall joints, a
heavy arched wooden door with iron hinges on the left third, the corridor
receding into deep teal darkness on the right.

CHARACTERS: in the center-right foreground, a stocky ginger-haired human
warrior in a white cloth tunic with blue trim, one hand raised in command. At
his side, a brown marsupial rat with alert red eyes, the size of a cat, loyal,
standing at the hero's side, no hostile pose. Next to it, a grey stone statue
mid-step, red glowing eyes, a very real sword in hand, loyal, standing at the
hero's side, no hostile pose. Beyond the doorway on the left, half-hidden in
shadow, a worker spider with a swollen orange abdomen, bloated like a living
bomb — clearly hostile, clearly not part of the squad.

LIGHTING: dramatic torchlight; warm amber and orange highlights on characters
and wall texture, cool teal and dark blue shadows, strong rim light, soft
bloom around flames, light floor fog catching the glow.

PALETTE: stone grey, sewage green, teal water, moss accents; no pure black,
no neon.

STYLE: crisp 16-bit pixel art, visible pixel clusters, deliberate dithering on
stone and in shadows, clean readable silhouettes, consistent pixel grid, sharp
edges, inspired by 1990s dungeon crawler key art.

COMPOSITION: cast on one third, threat and door on the opposite third, depth
toward the middle; top quarter dark, uncluttered, kept empty for logo.

CONSTRAINTS: no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no signature, no
photorealism, no 3D render, no anti-aliasing, no smooth gradients.
```

### Negative prompt

```
blurry, lowres, jpeg artifacts, text, watermark, signature, logo, letters,
numbers, photorealistic, 3d render, smooth gradients, antialiasing, deformed
hands, extra limbs, oversaturated, neon colors
```

### Params (Qwen-Image / Qwen3-Image)

- Resolution: 1664×832 landscape (2:1) — crop to 1024×500; square 1328×1328
  for feed posts (cast centered).
- Steps 30–40, CFG ~4.0 (Qwen-Image is tuned for low CFG — higher burns the
  pixel art), sampler euler; pixel-art LoRA optional at weight ≤0.6.
- Post-process: upscale 2× nearest neighbor, then downscale — never AI
  upscalers, they smooth pixels.
