# 32.4.beta.6 — Telegram announce (fixes present in beta.4 only; regressions that never shipped are excluded)

## EN

🗡️ **Remixed Dungeon 32.4.beta.6** — beta update!

🛠️ **Stability**
• Fixed two render-thread crashes (torn transitions, missing textures)
• No more silent freezes — the turn loop can no longer stall
• Failed saves no longer wedge the game
• Pets survive a crash mid-transition

⚖️ **Balance & gameplay**
• Statue gear is one-shot — no more infinite gear farm
• Split clones no longer copy gear — Multiplicity glyph dupe closed
• Dwarf King: his servants/undead no longer farm your token quest; he melees instead of endless summoning
• ShadowLord's maze can't be sealed to stall the boss
• Chess: board self-heals, pieces can't be resurrected, soft locks fixed
• Wand of Telekinesis discharges traps again
• Well of Transmutation keeps water for non-transmutable items
• Doors no longer slam on someone standing in them
• Flyers move through traps; Roots can't snare floating skulls; Cold Spirit is immune to Frost

Beta: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.nyrds.pixeldungeon.ml

## RU
