## EN — Discord

**⛪ 32.4.beta.17 — the Bishop's five trials**

• ✨ The Priest unlock is now a full quest line. Five trials from the Bishop in the Church: lay ten of the undead to rest; gather three seeds for the altar (sunflower or moongrace — the Bishop asks); show mercy — bring him a zombie or a bat you have tamed; strike at the source — destroy the death knights commanding the legions or free the enslaved souls; and finally banish the two succubi Hell sent after you.

• 😈 Priest vs demons: the anti-undead bonuses apply at half strength, and Sanctify burns demons for half damage. Eye, Scorpio, Succubus and the Imps count.
• 💥 Crash fixes: a save with an invalid hero record crashed on every launch — the game now falls back to the title screen and the run stays reachable; the chess minigame no longer crashes when processing a win; a mod script crash no longer kills the running game frame.
