## EN — Discord

**⚒️ 32.4.beta.16 — Remixed RPG rides again**

• 💥 Crash fix: since beta.8 the Remixed RPG mod crashed the game the moment you chose smithing materials at the blacksmith's or picked arrows for your bow. Our compatibility break, our fix — the mod works again.
• ✨ Early preview: the Priest class is playable. To unlock — complete the Bishop's quest in the Church: lay 15 undead to rest.
• 🙏 Divine spells, cast for mana: Blessing (attack and defense improved for a while), Holy Water (scalds and slows the undead in a splash), Exorcism (heavy holy damage to one foe, doubled against the undead), Last Rites (burns all undead within two cells and saps their strength).
• 🕊️ Battle vestments: Sanctify — a holy burst that burns the undead nearby and blesses the priest.
• ⚔️ +25% damage to the undead, 25% less damage taken from them.
• 🎒 Starts with a quarterstaff, 12 Strength, 12 mana, Blessing in the quickslot and a Scroll of Remove Curse already identified.

Early preview — expect balancing: numbers and details may still change. No subclasses for the priest, same as the necromancer, gnoll and doctor.
As usual — beta: old saves work.
